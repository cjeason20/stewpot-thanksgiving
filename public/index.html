Stewpot's Thanksgiving Celebration

This Thanksgiving, we're doing something a little different! Stewpot's Community Kitchen is transforming the traditional Thanksgiving meal into a special dining experience, complete with table service, a menu, and plenty of holiday hospitality. Because everyone deserves a seat at the table!

This year, we're bringing the community together in a special way at Stewpot's Community Kitchen. Volunteers will help serve a restaurant-style Thanksgiving meal, and each volunteer will bring a dish to share.
